PRICE NEGOTIATION & VALUE SELLING RUBRIC

Purpose
Evaluate how effectively the sales representative handled pricing discussions and reinforced business value.

==================================================
WHAT TO LOOK FOR
==================================================

- Reinforcement of ROI and business value
- Clarifying questions about pricing concerns
- Confidence during pricing discussions
- Strategic handling of discounts or concessions

==================================================
WHAT COUNTS AS GOOD
==================================================

- Rep explores the reason behind pricing concerns
- Pricing is tied to business outcomes and value
- Rep avoids unnecessary discounting

==================================================
WHAT COUNTS AS POOR
==================================================

- Rep offers discounts too early
- Pricing concerns are handled defensively
- Little or no value reinforcement before concessions

==================================================
HOW TO SCORE
==================================================

- 5 = Strong negotiation discipline and value selling
- 3 = Moderate negotiation effectiveness
- 1 = Severe price caving and weak value positioning

==================================================
COMMON FAILURE PATTERNS
==================================================

- Immediate discount offers
- Weak ROI discussions
- Failing to probe for underlying concerns
- Over-focusing on price instead of business impact

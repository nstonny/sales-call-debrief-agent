OVERPITCHING & DISCOVERY BALANCE RUBRIC

Purpose
Evaluate whether the sales representative balanced customer discovery with product pitching.

==================================================
WHAT TO LOOK FOR
==================================================

- Open-ended discovery questions
- Exploration of customer pain points
- Balanced participation between rep and customer
- Product discussion tied to customer needs

==================================================
WHAT COUNTS AS GOOD
==================================================

- Rep listens actively and asks meaningful follow-up questions
- Customer speaks frequently and shares business challenges
- Product explanations are concise and relevant

==================================================
WHAT COUNTS AS POOR
==================================================

- Rep dominates the conversation with feature-heavy pitching
- Product discussion starts before understanding customer needs
- Customer engagement is low or disengaged

==================================================
HOW TO SCORE
==================================================

- 5 = Excellent discovery and balanced conversation
- 3 = Moderate discovery with some overpitching
- 1 = Severe overpitching and weak customer understanding

==================================================
COMMON FAILURE PATTERNS
==================================================

- Long feature monologues
- Generic pitching without business context
- Ignoring customer cues or concerns

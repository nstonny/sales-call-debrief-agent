DISCOVERY CALL RUBRIC

Purpose
Evaluate how effectively the sales representative uncovered customer needs, pain points, and business priorities.

==================================================
WHAT TO LOOK FOR
==================================================

- Open-ended discovery questions
- Exploration of customer pain points and workflows
- Discussion of business impact or priorities
- Active listening and meaningful follow-up questions

==================================================
WHAT COUNTS AS GOOD
==================================================

- Customer speaks frequently and shares detailed challenges
- Rep uncovers operational or business pain points
- Conversation feels collaborative and customer-focused

==================================================
WHAT COUNTS AS POOR
==================================================

- Rep asks mostly shallow or yes/no questions
- Product pitching starts too early
- Customer needs are not clearly understood

==================================================
HOW TO SCORE
==================================================

- 5 = Excellent discovery and deep customer understanding
- 3 = Moderate discovery with missed opportunities
- 1 = Poor discovery and minimal customer understanding

==================================================
COMMON FAILURE PATTERNS
==================================================

- Rushing into demos or features too early
- Weak follow-up questioning
- Minimal discussion of business impact
- Making assumptions without validation

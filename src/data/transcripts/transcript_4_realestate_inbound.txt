SALES CALL TRANSCRIPT 4
========================
Industry: Commercial Real Estate
Call Type: Inbound — Warm Lead
Outcome: Meeting booked, strong pipeline
Duration: 14 minutes
Rep: Jason Brauer
Prospect: Anika Patel, Founder, Patel & Co. Consulting (18 employees)
========================

Jason: Hi Anika, this is Jason from Harmon Commercial Properties. You reached out through our website about office space in the downtown district — is this a good time?

Anika: Yes, perfect timing actually.

Jason: Great. So I saw you filled out the form about two hours ago — was there something specific that triggered the search today?

Anika: Yeah, we just signed a new client that's going to require us to bring on about six or seven more people over the next four months. We're currently in a co-working space and it works fine for 18, but at 25 we're going to be falling over each other.

Jason: Understood. And is the downtown location a must-have, or is that a preference?

Anika: Strong preference. Most of our clients are downtown, we do a lot of in-person meetings, and honestly my team likes working down there.

Jason: Got it. What's your current footprint — square footage-wise?

Anika: I have no idea, honestly. It's a WeWork. We have two medium rooms and some hot desks.

Jason: No worries. For 18 people, you're probably at around 2,500 to 3,000 square feet give or take. If you're planning for 25, you're looking at somewhere between 3,500 and 4,200 depending on how you use the space — conference rooms, open plan, private offices, that kind of thing.

Anika: Right. We'd definitely need a couple of proper meeting rooms. Client-facing, so they need to look professional.

Jason: Absolutely. And budget — are you comfortable talking about that?

Anika: I'd want to keep it under $15,000 a month all in. Ideally closer to $12,000.

Jason: That's a workable range for what you're describing. There's actually a space that just came available on Merchant Street — 3,800 square feet, already fitted out, eight private offices and an open bullpen. It was a law firm so it's very polished. Asking is $13,200 a month. And because it's move-in ready, you could be in there in four to six weeks.

Anika: Oh, that's fast. That's actually helpful. Can we see it?

Jason: I can arrange a viewing this week — Thursday or Friday work?

Anika: Thursday afternoon.

Jason: Done. I'll send you the full spec sheet and some photos before then so you can get a feel for it. A couple of things to prep for the viewing — do you have a sense of how long a lease you'd want? These buildings typically want a minimum of two years.

Anika: Two years is probably fine. We're not going anywhere.

Jason: Great. And is it just you making the decision, or should I expect others at the viewing?

Anika: My office manager will come. She handles a lot of the day-to-day and I trust her eye for this kind of thing.

Jason: Perfect. I'll plan for two of you. Alright Anika, I'll get that info over to you within the hour. Looking forward to Thursday.

Anika: Same. Thanks Jason.

SALES CALL TRANSCRIPT 10
========================
Industry: HR Consulting / Outsourced HR Services
Call Type: Discovery Call
Rep Mistakes: Asks leading questions, puts words in prospect's mouth, jumps to solution before understanding problem, misses key signals
Outcome: Prospect agrees to next step but with low intent — deal later falls through
Duration: 24 minutes
Rep: Natalie Cross
Prospect: Paul Drummond, Managing Director, Drummond Recruitment Ltd (55 employees)
========================

Natalie: Hi Paul, thanks for jumping on. So I know you're probably dealing with all the usual HR headaches — compliance, retention, that kind of thing. That's usually why people come to us. Is that what's going on for you?

Paul: Uh, sort of, yeah.

Natalie: Right, exactly. And I imagine with a team your size, you probably don't have a dedicated HR person, so things fall through the cracks, right?

Paul: We do actually have someone. Sarah handles HR.

Natalie: Oh great, sure. And she's probably stretched thin, right? One person trying to do everything?

Paul: She manages fine, mostly.

Natalie: Of course, of course. But there must be some areas where she's struggling to keep up. Compliance is a big one — employment law changes all the time. Are you confident you're fully up to date?

Paul: I mean, we haven't had any issues.

Natalie: Right, but the risk is always there, you know? One tribunal case can cost you tens of thousands. That's the kind of thing our clients come to us to avoid. Is that something that keeps you up at night?

Paul: Not particularly, no.

Natalie: Okay, sure. What about retention? Are you losing people at the moment?

Paul: Our turnover is pretty low actually. We've had a couple of people leave this year but that's normal.

Natalie: Totally. And when people leave, do you do exit interviews? Because a lot of companies miss the signals and then wonder why people keep leaving.

Paul: We do exit interviews, yeah.

Natalie: Perfect. So you're doing that. What about recruitment — finding good people is harder than ever right now, isn't it?

Paul: We're hiring for two roles at the moment, yeah.

Natalie: Exactly, and I'm sure it's taking longer than you'd like and costing more than you'd want. That's a big one. Our recruitment support module is something clients find really valuable. Can I show you how that works?

Paul: Sure, I guess.

Natalie: Great. So... [product walkthrough, ~10 minutes] ...and that's essentially how it works. Does that solve the problem you're facing?

Paul: Yeah, looks interesting.

Natalie: Brilliant. So I think we should get a full proposal over to you. What's your budget looking like?

Paul: I'd need to understand the cost before I could say.

Natalie: Of course, of course. I'll put something together and we can go from there. Shall we say same time next week?

Paul: Sure, yeah.

[Follow-up note: Paul did not attend the next meeting. His EA rescheduled twice, then he replied saying they were "not ready to look at this right now." No clear pain was ever established. Natalie led the entire conversation and assumed problems that did not exist.]

CALL TYPE: Solution Evaluation Call

Company: Acme Manufacturing
Rep: Sarah Johnson
Prospect: Michael Brown, VP Operations

--------------------------------------------------
CONTEXT
--------------------------------------------------

The prospect has completed an initial discovery process and is evaluating vendors.

The prospect has expressed interest in the solution but has concerns about cost, implementation effort, and internal approval.

--------------------------------------------------
BUDGET OBJECTION
--------------------------------------------------

Prospect:
I like what I've seen so far, but the price is higher than we expected.

Rep:
I appreciate you bringing that up. Can you help me understand what specifically concerns you about the investment?

Prospect:
It's roughly 25% higher than another vendor we're considering.

Rep:
Understood. Besides price, what factors are most important in your evaluation?

Prospect:
Ease of implementation and reporting capabilities.

Rep:
Earlier you mentioned that reporting delays cost the business approximately €250,000 per quarter. Is that still accurate?

Prospect:
Yes.

Rep:
If we could significantly reduce those delays, would the business impact outweigh the difference in purchase price?

Prospect:
Potentially, yes.

Rep:
Would it be helpful if we worked through a business case together?

Prospect:
Yes, that would help.

--------------------------------------------------
COMPETITOR OBJECTION
--------------------------------------------------

Prospect:
Another vendor is offering a lower price and claims similar functionality.

Rep:
That's fair. What do you like most about their solution?

Prospect:
The cost and the simplicity of their proposal.

Rep:
What concerns do you have about their approach?

Prospect:
Their integration capabilities seem limited.

Rep:
How important are integrations to your long-term goals?

Prospect:
Very important. We don't want another disconnected system.

Rep:
Would it be useful to compare the long-term operational impact of both approaches?

Prospect:
Yes.

Rep:
Let's review those differences together.

--------------------------------------------------
IMPLEMENTATION RISK OBJECTION
--------------------------------------------------

Prospect:
I'm worried implementation could disrupt our operations.

Rep:
That's a common concern. Can you tell me more about what specifically worries you?

Prospect:
We don't have a large IT team.

Rep:
Understood.

Rep:
Would it be helpful if I shared examples of customers with similar resource constraints?

Prospect:
Definitely.

Rep:
Several manufacturing customers completed implementation in under eight weeks with minimal internal resources.

Prospect:
That's reassuring.

Rep:
Would a conversation with one of those customers be valuable?

Prospect:
Yes.

--------------------------------------------------
TIMING OBJECTION
--------------------------------------------------

Prospect:
We're interested, but this may not be the right time.

Rep:
Can you help me understand what's driving the timing concern?

Prospect:
Several initiatives are competing for attention.

Rep:
That makes sense.

Rep:
If this project were delayed by six months, what impact would that have on the business?

Prospect:
We would likely continue experiencing reporting delays and inefficiencies.

Rep:
Do you estimate that would have a financial impact?

Prospect:
Yes.

Rep:
Approximately how much?

Prospect:
Probably several hundred thousand euros.

Rep:
Given that impact, how should we think about the cost of waiting?

Prospect:
That's a fair question.

--------------------------------------------------
AUTHORITY OBJECTION
--------------------------------------------------

Prospect:
Ultimately, our CFO will need to approve this.

Rep:
That's helpful to know.

Rep:
What factors will be most important to your CFO?

Prospect:
Financial return and implementation risk.

Rep:
Would it make sense to schedule a discussion where we can review projected ROI and implementation planning?

Prospect:
Yes.

Rep:
I'd be happy to prepare those materials.

--------------------------------------------------
NEXT STEPS
--------------------------------------------------

Rep:
It sounds like the key concerns are investment level, implementation risk, and CFO approval.

Prospect:
That's correct.

Rep:
To help with those areas, I'll prepare:
- A business case analysis
- ROI projections
- Customer references
- Implementation planning materials

Prospect:
That would be very useful.

Rep:
Let's review those with your CFO next week.

Prospect:
Sounds good.
